# Third-party models and code

Training and evaluation depend on the following public models or projects. The
release bundles do not duplicate their unmodified base weights:

- Ultralytics YOLO26x
- `IDEA-Research/grounding-dino-tiny`
- `nvidia/LocateAnything-3B` and NVLabs Eagle
- `Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B`, `Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B`, `Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B`, and the 27B baseline
- `OpenGVLab/InternVL3_5-8B-HF`, `OpenGVLab/InternVL3_5-14B-HF`, and
  `OpenGVLab/InternVL3_5-38B-HF`
- `mistral-community/pixtral-12b`
- `google/gemma-3-4b-it`, `google/gemma-3-12b-it`, and
  `google/gemma-3-27b-it`

Fine-tuned weights, adapters, and compatibility patches do not replace or
modify the upstream terms. Before publication, the publisher must verify each
upstream license, attribution requirement, redistribution scope, and use
restriction. LocateAnything materials remain limited to non-commercial
research or evaluation under their upstream terms.
